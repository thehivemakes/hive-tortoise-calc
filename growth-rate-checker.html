How to Read a Tortoise Growth Checkpoint

Sulcata tortoises are hatchlings around 1.5-2.5 inches, add roughly 2-4 inches of shell length in their first year, and commonly reach 8-15 inches by age 5 and 15-20 inches by age 10 before leveling off as adults in the 18-30 inch range, according to tortoise-keeping references.

Hermann's tortoises grow more slowly and stay much smaller: hatchlings are typically 1-2 inches, reaching roughly 2-3 inches at one year, 3-3.5 inches at two years, and 5-7 inches by six years in commonly cited ranges.

Russian tortoises are the smallest of the three and their growth data is coarser in most keeping references \u2014 hatchlings start around 1-1.5 inches and gain roughly 1-2 inches per year until adults settle around 5-6 inches (males) to 7-10 inches (females). This checker uses a combined adult range since it does not ask for sex.
