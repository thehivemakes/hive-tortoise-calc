About TortoiseCalc

TortoiseCalc is a free enclosure size calculator for pet tortoise and box turtle keepers. Enter shell length, tortoise count, life stage, and housing type to get a minimum floor-area guideline before buying a tank, table, or building an outdoor pen.

The calculator runs entirely in your browser — no account, no data leaving your device. Share a result by URL, or bookmark it to recheck as your tortoise grows.

Free tortoise enclosure sizing tool. No signup, no tracking. General husbandry guidelines only, not species-specific or veterinary advice.
